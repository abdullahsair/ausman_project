Choosing the Right Fabric for Varsity Jackets

When it comes to outfitting a university class, sports team, or alumni network, nothing quite matches the timeless appeal of a custom varsity jacket. However, the secret to a premium jacket doesn't just lie in the embroidered logos—it lies fundamentally in the choice of materials.

1. The Body: Melton Wool vs. Cotton Fleece

Traditionally, the body of a high-quality varsity jacket is made from Melton Wool. This tightly woven fabric is incredibly durable, wind-resistant, and provides that heavy, premium feel associated with luxury institutional merch.

For regions with warmer climates, or for a more budget-friendly approach, Heavyweight Cotton Fleece (typically around 320-400 GSM) is an excellent alternative. It offers a softer drape and is much easier to wash, making it perfect for everyday student wear.

2. The Sleeves: Genuine Leather vs. PU Leather

The contrast sleeves are the hallmark of the varsity style.

  • Genuine Cowhide Leather: The absolute gold standard. It breaks in beautifully over time, lasts decades, and gives the jacket significant weight. Ideal for premium executive or alumni gifting.
  • PU (Polyurethane) Leather: A synthetic, vegan-friendly alternative. Modern PU leathers can mimic the texture of real leather quite well, significantly reducing the cost and weight of the garment.

3. The Details: Ribbing, Snaps, and Lining

Don't overlook the trim! The collar, cuffs, and hem should utilize a high-density 1x1 or 2x2 acrylic/cotton ribbing to prevent stretching over time. For the closure, heavy-duty brass or enamel-coated snap buttons ensure longevity compared to cheap plastic snaps.

Finally, a quilted polyester lining on the inside not only adds warmth but provides a sleek, finished look that screams high-end manufacturing.

Conclusion

Your choice of materials will dictate the lifespan, perception, and cost of your institutional jackets. At Ausman, we source directly from top-tier mills to ensure whether you choose Melton wool or heavy fleece, the end result is spectacular.
